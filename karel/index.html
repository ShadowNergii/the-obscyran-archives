The First Continent

Karel

Every journey begins somewhere.

For generations, Karel has been the homeland of PokéCyrus Online. Its towns, routes, caves, and cities are where countless Trainers first began their adventures.

Karel is the first playable continent in PokéCyrus Online and serves as the foundation of the restored world. Returning players will rediscover familiar places brought back with modern production standards, while new players will experience them for the very first time.

Operation: Karel Reborn is dedicated to faithfully restoring this beloved continent while preserving the identity that made it unforgettable.

Beyond Karel lies the wider world of Obscyra. But every Chronicle begins here.

The Journey

Players begin their adventure in Karel. They receive their first Pokémon, explore the restored continent, challenge the Archive System, uncover ancient mysteries, and slowly discover that Karel is only one continent within the far larger world of Obscyra.

As their Chronicle grows, so too does the world around them.
